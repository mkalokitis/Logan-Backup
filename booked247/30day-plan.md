# Booked 247 - 30-Day Sprint Plan

**Goal:** First paid customer in 30 days
**Budget:** $0 (organic only)
**Focus:** Junk removal, Hamilton NJ area

---

## Week 1: Build Foundation

- [ ] Build site in GHL with AI builder (use prompt I gave)
- [ ] Set up GHL pipeline for incoming leads
- [ ] Create Google Business profile (if needed)
- [ ] Finalize prospect list (50 businesses)

**Deliverable:** Live site with 3 packages live

---

## Week 2: Outreach Sprint

**Day 8-10:** DM 50 junk removal businesses
**Day 11-12:** Post in local Facebook groups (Hamilton, Mercer County, local home service groups)
**Day 13-14:** Follow up on all DM responses

**Script:** See `/workspace/booked247/outreach-scripts.md`

**Goal:** 10+ conversations started

---

## Week 3: Demo & Close

**Day 15-17:** Take every call/demo request
**Day 18-19:** First offer at discount if needed ($297 setup instead of $697)
**Day 20-21:** Deliver first installation

**Goal:** Close first customer at ANY price to prove the model

---

## Week 4: Refine & Scale

**Day 22-24:** Ask for referrals + testimonials
**Day 25-27:** Refine pitch based on objections heard
**Day 28-30:** Double outreach volume

**Goal:** 3+ paying customers OR clear signal on pivot

---

## Daily Routine (Week 2+)

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| Morning | Send 10 DMs + 5 emails |
| Mid-day | Follow up on previous day's messages |
| Evening | Update prospect tracker |

---

## Key Metrics to Track

- DMs sent
- Responses received
- Demo calls scheduled
- Demo calls completed
- Proposals sent
- Customers closed

---

## First Customer Wins

When you get the first paying customer:

1. Document everything you did
2. Get a testimonial (video preferred)
3. Ask for referrals ("Who else do you know in junk removal?")
4. Replicate what worked
