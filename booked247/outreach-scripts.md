## Objection Handling

**"I already have a system"**
> Cool, what do you use? (Listen) And how's it working for you? Do you ever miss calls?

**"It's too expensive"**
> I hear you. But here's the thing — one missed job pays for 2-3 months of this service. What's your average job worth? $200? $300? That's one call back.

**"I don't have time for this"**
> That's exactly why we exist. We set it up, it runs itself. You just get the texts and show up to the job.
