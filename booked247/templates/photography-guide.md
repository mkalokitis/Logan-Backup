## PART 1: PHOTOGRAPHY BASICS

### LIGHTING — The #1 Most Important Thing

**Natural light is your best friend.**

| What to Do | Why |
|------------|-----|
| Shoot near windows or outdoors | Natural light flatters subjects |
| Face the light source | Avoids shadow on face |
| Avoid overhead lights | Creates raccoon eyes |
| Golden hour (sunrise/sunset) | Warmest, most flattering light |
| Cloudy days = perfect outdoor light | Soft, even lighting |

**Good vs Bad Light:**
- ✅ Window light, outdoors in shade, golden hour
- ❌ Harsh midday sun, overhead fluorescent, backlight without reflector

---

### ANGLES — Make It Look Professional

**Property Photos:**

| Angle | Best For |
|-------|----------|
| 45-degree corner shot | Full room view |
| Eye-level for details | Countertops, features |
| Wide shot | Entire room |
| Vertical for online | Listings look better vertical |
| Bird's eye (from doorway) | Bedrooms, living rooms |

**Selfies/Team Photos:**

| Angle | Best For |
|-------|----------|
| Eye-level or slightly above | Headshots, team photos |
| Hold phone at eye level | Avoids double chin |
| Slight angle (not straight on) | More dynamic |
| Sit at a slight angle | Looks slimmer, more natural |

---

### COMPOSITION — The Rule of Thirds

**Imagine a 3x3 grid on your screen:**

```
|---|---|---|
|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
|   | X |   |  <-- Put subject's eyes here
|---|---|---|
|   |   |   |
```

- Put your subject (or main object) in the left or right third
- Leave "room" for them to look into
- Don't center everything

## PART 2: PHONE SETTINGS

### Must-Change Settings:

1. **Turn ON Grid Lines**
   - Settings → Camera → Grid
   - Helps you align shots

2. **Set to Highest Resolution**
   - Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible

3. **Turn OFF Auto-Flash**
   - Auto flash ruins photos
   - Use natural light instead

4. **Shoot in Portrait Mode** (for people)
   - Blurs background, looks professional

---

### Quick Camera Settings Checklist:

- [ ] Grid lines ON
- [ ] HDR ON (for landscapes)
- [ ] Auto-Flash OFF
- [ ] Portrait mode available
- [ ] 4K video for Reels

## PART 3: WHAT TO PHOTO

### Property Shots (Priority Order):

1. **Front of house** — Wide shot, clean approach
2. **Living room** — Bright, inviting
3. **Kitchen** — Counter clear, staging ideal
4. **Primary bedroom** — Made bed, neat
5. **Backyard/outdoor** — Clean, furniture staged
6. **Bathrooms** — Sparkling, fresh towels
7. **Garage/basement** — Clean, organized

### Personal Brand Photos:

1. **At a listing** — You with the sold sign
2. **Team photos** — Authentic, not too staged
3. **Behind the scenes** — Loading, staging, meeting
4. **Local landmarks** — Show you know the area
5. **Client celebrations** — Handing over keys

## PART 4: EDITING APPS & TOOLS

### Free Editing Apps (Use These):

| App | Best For | Free Features |
|-----|----------|---------------|
| **Snapseed** | Quick edits, filters | All basic features |
| **Canva** | Text overlays, templates | Lots of free templates |
| **Lightroom Mobile** | Color correction | Basic presets |
| **Picsart** | Object removal | Basic editing |

### Best Workflow:

1. **Shoot in original** — Don't filter while shooting
2. **Edit in Snapseed** — Fix lighting first
3. **Add text in Canva** — If needed
4. **Export** — Post

---

### Editing Steps (In Order):

**Step 1: Fix Light**
- Brightness up slightly
- Shadows up slightly
- Avoid over-editing

**Step 2: Fix Color**
- Warmth up slightly (makes it inviting)
- Saturation slightly up
- Don't make it neon

**Step 3: Crop**
- Remove distractions
- Follow rule of thirds
- Straighten the horizon

**Step 4: Add Style (Optional)**
- Use a subtle filter
- Don't over-filter
- Should still look real

## PART 5: QUICK REFERENCE CARDS

### Property Photo Checklist:

- [ ] Front exterior — clean approach, good light
- [ ] Living room — bright, TV off, personal items hidden
- [ ] Kitchen — counters clear, fridge clean
- [ ] Primary bedroom — made bed, neat
- [ ] Bathroom — sparkling, fresh towels
- [ ] Backyard — furniture staged if possible
- [ ] Use portrait mode for rooms
- [ ] Turn off all lights for natural light only

### Selfie/Brand Photo Checklist:

- [ ] Good lighting (window or outdoors)
- [ ] Grid aligned (eyes on third line)
- [ ] Clean background
- [ ] Not too close (shows chin)
- [ ] Smile — real, not forced
- [ ] Sunglasses off (unless brand)

### Video/Reel Checklist:

- [ ] Hold phone horizontal for Reels
- [ ] Good audio (avoid wind)
- [ ] Steady — use tripod or still
- [ ] 6-15 seconds long
- [ ] Bright lighting

## PART 6: MINIMAL EQUIPMENT NEEDED

### What You Actually Need ($50 or Less):

1. **Your phone** — That's it
2. **Phone tripod ($15-25)** — For stable shots
3. **Ring light ($20)** — Indoor shoots
4. **Cleaning supplies** — Clean the lens!

### Nice to Have (Not Required):
- Drone (for aerial property shots)
- DSLR camera (not needed)
- Professional lighting (overkill)

## SUMMARY: THE 10-MINUTE RULE

You don't need hours. Just 10 minutes:

- **Morning:** Quick property photo (5 min)
- **After showing:** Grab a shot (2 min)
- **Weekly:** Batch 5-10 photos (10 min)

Then edit and post later.
