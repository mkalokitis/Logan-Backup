## SCORING EXPLAINED

### Posting Consistency (20 pts)
- 15-20 pts: 3-5 posts/week consistently
- 10-14 pts: 1-2 posts/week
- 5-9 pts: Occasional posting
- 0-4 pts: Rarely/none

### Engagement Rate (20 pts)
- 15-20 pts: 3%+ engagement
- 10-14 pts: 1-3% engagement
- 5-9 pts: 0.5-1% engagement
- 0-4 pts: Below 0.5%

### Content Quality (20 pts)
- 15-20 pts: Mix of educational + personal + social proof
- 10-14 pts: Mostly promotional or mostly personal
- 5-9 pts: Low variety, generic content
- 0-4 pts: No clear strategy

### Profile Optimization (15 pts)
- 12-15 pts: Clear bio, CTA in every post, link in bio
- 8-11 pts: Some elements present
- 4-7 pts: Missing most elements
- 0-3 pts: No optimization

### Video/Story Presence (10 pts)
- 8-10 pts: Active on Reels + Stories
- 5-7 pts: Some video content
- 2-4 pts: Rarely posts video
- 0-1 pts: No video content

### Audience Engagement (15 pts)
- 12-15 pts: Replies to comments, engages back
- 8-11 pts: Sometimes engages
- 4-7 pts: Rarely engages
- 0-3 pts: Ignores audience
