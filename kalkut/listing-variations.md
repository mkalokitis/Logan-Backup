## PART 3: MESSAGE RESPONSE SCRIPTS

### Script 1 - Get Details (Default)

> Hey! Thanks for reaching out. Can you send me photos of what you need taken? Also, what's the address and about how much stuff do you have?

---

### Script 2 - Quick Qualify (Rental)

> Hi! Do you want to rent the trailer to haul it yourself, or do you need us to pick it up? Also, what's the address and about how much are you looking to haul?

---

### Script 3 - Upsell Rental → Junk Removal

> We can actually pick it up for you too — if you don't want to haul it yourself. Want me to give you a price for junk removal instead?

---

### Script 4 - Get Price Fast

> Got it. Send over the address and how much stuff — I'll get you a price. When do you need it gone?

---

### Script 5 - Booking It

> Cool. You're looking at [price]. I can do [date] for pickup. Sound good? I'll text you to confirm day-of.

---

### Quick Qualify Questions (before quoting)

1. "What's the address?" (know your area)
2. "How much stuff?" (small/medium/van load/full load)
3. "When do you need it?" (today/this week/scheduled)
4. "Any heavy items?" (appliances/hot tubs — may need extra help)

---

### Transition from Rental to Removal

When someone asks about trailer rental:

> "You can also just tell us what needs to go — we'll pick it up. Easier than hauling it yourself. Want a price for junk removal instead?"
