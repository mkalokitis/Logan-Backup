# Task 3: Lead Monetization Strategy

## Lead Type 1: Dump Trailer Inquiry

### How to Respond

1. Ask: "Do you want to rent the trailer, or do you need us to pick it up?"
2. If they want pickup → pivot to junk removal pricing
3. If rental → quote $99/day (or your rate)

### How to Price

| Load Size | Your Price |
|-----------|-------------|
| Small (few bags/debris) | $99/day |
| Medium (car load) | $129/day |
| Large (truck/trailer load) | $149/day |
| Extra large (multiple trailer loads) | Call for pricing |

**Add-on fees:**
- Delivery over 15 miles: $25
- Weekend pickup: +$25
- Heavy debris (concrete/dirt): +$20

### When to Upsell

- They mention "it's heavy" → offer pickup instead of rental
- They seem unsure → "We can just come take it — easier for you"
- They have a lot → "If it's more than a trailer load, we do full junk removal by the load"

### When to Route

- Outside your service area → refer to another hauler
- Too large/complex (multi-day project) → refer to contractor
- You can't deliver in time → refer to competitor

## Lead Type 2: Junk Removal Inquiry

### How to Respond

1. Get photos + address (must-have)
2. Qualify: "How much stuff?" + "When do you need it?"
3. Quote based on load size

### How to Price

| Load Size | Your Price |
|-----------|-------------|
| Small (chair/mattress/appliance) | $50-$80 |
| Medium (car load/van load) | $100-$150 |
| Large (pickup truck load) | $175-$225 |
| Extra large (multiple loads) | $250+ |

**Factors that increase price:**
- Basement/garage cleanout (harder to access)
- Heavy items (pianos, hot tubs, safes) — +$50+
- stairs (more than 1 flight) — +$25
- Distance outside Hamilton area — +$20-50

### When to Upsell

- They have a trailer → "We also do trailer rental if you want to DIY"
- They're cleaning out a property → "Need dump trailer too?"
- They mention other jobs → "What else needs doing?"

### When to Route

- Outside service area → sell lead to other junk removal ($20)
- Job too big → bring in partner, split revenue
- You don't have capacity → sell lead

## Lead Routing Quick Guide

| Scenario | Action |
|----------|--------|
| Lead in your area + you can take it | Quote and book |
| Lead outside your area | Text to partner junk remover for $20 |
| Lead bigger than you can handle | Quote high or bring partner |
| Lead too small (one item) | Take it — $50 minimum |
