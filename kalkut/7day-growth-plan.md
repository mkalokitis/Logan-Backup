## Realistic Goals for Week 1

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Messages received | 10-20 |
| Quotes sent | 8-15 |
| Jobs booked | 2-5 |
| Revenue | $200-500 |

The goal isn't perfection — it's to get the system moving and figure out what works.

---

## What to Do If Nothing Happens

If after 3 days you get no messages:
1. Change your listing titles — more specific ("Same-Day" vs "Fast")
2. Add more variations — try different hooks
3. Post in more groups
4. Lower prices on rental if needed

Don't give up — test, tweak, repeat.
