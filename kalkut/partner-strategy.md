# Task 5: Partner Strategy - Build Your Referral Network

## Why You Need Partners

Some leads you can't take:
- Outside your area
- Too big for you
- You don't have capacity

Instead of saying "sorry, we can't help" → **flip the lead and get paid.**

---

## Who to Partner With

| Partner Type | What They Do | Why They Need You |
|--------------|--------------|-------------------|
| Other junk removal companies | Take overflow/area leads | Lead flow they don't have |
| Contractors | Framing, demo, renovation | Generates debris/cleanup jobs |
| Property managers | Manage rentals/estates | Need junk removal regularly |
| Real estate agents | Selling homes | Pre-moveout cleanouts |
| HOA managers | Community maintenance | Ongoing junk pickup needs |

---

## How to Pitch Junk Removal Partners

### The Message

> Hey [Name], this is Mark with Kalkut Designs. I'm local to Hamilton and do junk removal and dump trailer rentals.
>
> Here's the thing: sometimes I get calls from [their area] or jobs bigger than I can handle, and I hate turning away leads.
>
> Would you be open to me sending you leads I can't take? I'd send them your way and you'd pay me $20 per lead — or if the job books over $200, 10% of the total.
>
> No cost to you to start — just see if the leads turn into jobs. What do you think?

---

### Key Points When Pitching

1. **Free to start** — "No cost to you to try"
2. **Easy money** — "You're already doing the job anyway"
3. **No competition** — "I'm not competing with you — I'm in a different area"
4. **Mutual benefit** — "You get leads you'd otherwise miss"

---

## How to Pitch Contractors

### The Message

> Hey [Name], this is Mark with Kalkut Designs. I'm local to Hamilton and do junk removal and dump trailer rentals.
>
> Whenever I do a demo or renovation job, there's always debris left behind. Sometimes it's more than my truck can handle.
>
> I'm looking to build a referral network — if you need junk removal on a job site, I can send you a quote. And if you ever get a customer who needs junk hauled before/after your work, I'd love the referral.
>
> Not looking to compete with you — just want to help your customers get the job done.
>
> Let's keep it simple — if you refer me a job that books, I'll give you 10% of the total. Sound fair?

---

## Payment Structure

| Lead Type | You Pay Partner | Partner Pays You |
|-----------|-----------------|------------------|
| Basic lead (any size) | $20 | — |
| Job books over $200 | 10% of job | — |
| Partner refers you a job | — | 10% of job total |

**Simple rule:** If you refer → you get paid. If they refer → they get paid.

## How to Find Partners

### Quick Search
Search for:
- "junk removal [nearby town]"
- "hauling [your county]"
- "dumpster rental [your area]"
- "contractor [your area]"
- "property management [your area]"

### Where They Advertise
- Facebook Marketplace
- Facebook groups
- Google Maps
- Craigslist

### What to Say
Just DM them the pitch. Keep it short. Most won't reply — that's fine. You need 3-5 good partners, not 50.

## Partner Script Quick Reference

### Opening
> Hey, I'm Mark with Kalkut Designs. Local junk removal company.

### Pitch
> I sometimes get leads outside my area or too big for my truck. Instead of turning them away, I like to send them to other local guys. We could swap leads — if I can't take it, I send it to you, and vice versa.

### Payment
> For any lead I send that books a job, I'd pay you $20. Or if it's a big job over $200, 10% of the total. Simple as that.

### Close
> Worth a try? No risk to you — you only get leads you'd otherwise miss.
