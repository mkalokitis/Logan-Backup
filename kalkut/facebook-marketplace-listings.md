## Tips for Marketplace

1. **Post in the morning** (7-9am) — More views
2. **Bump your posts** — Refresh them every 2-3 days to stay at the top
3. **Respond fast** — Within 30 min if possible
4. **Use real photos** — Of your truck, trailer, cleanup jobs (I'll help you get photos later)
5. **Get their address first** — Before quoting, so you know if it's in your area

## How to Flip Leads You Can't Take

When you get a lead outside your area or too big to handle:

> "Hey — we're actually booked up in [their area], but I've got a guy I can refer you to. Want me to connect you?"

Then text the lead to another junk removal company:
> "Hey, got you a lead in [area] — [customer name], needs [item type]. They're ready to book. $20 if you take it."

Or charge them the lead fee directly.
