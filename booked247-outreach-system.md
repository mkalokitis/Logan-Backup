## OUTREACH WORKFLOW

### STEP 1: Build Lead List

**Find local businesses using:**
- Google Maps searches
- Yelp listings
- Angi/HomeAdvisor
- Facebook local groups

**For each business, collect:**
- Business name
- Owner name (if possible)
- Email (from website or directory)
- Phone
- Service area

---

### STEP 2: Send Cold Email

**Email 1 (The Hook)**

```
Subject: Quick question about [BUSINESS NAME]

Hi [NAME],

I noticed you guys do [SERVICE] in [AREA]. Hope business is good.

I work with local businesses like yours who want more leads from their online presence.

Quick question — are you currently doing any content marketing or social media? Or is that something you wish you had time for?

No pitch intended — just curious what's working for you.

Best,
[YOUR NAME]
Booked 247
```

---

**Email 2 (If No Reply)**

```
Subject: Following up — social media for [AREA] businesses

Hi [NAME],

Just wanted to bump this — hoping it didn't get lost.

I've been helping local [BUSINESS TYPE] get more visibility online. Simple stuff — consistent posting, content ideas, newsletters.

Curious — is this something you'd be open to chatting about? No pressure either way.

Thanks,
[YOUR NAME]
```

---

**Email 3 (Soft Close)**

```
Subject: One more try — and no more emails after this

Hey [NAME],

I'll stop bothering you after this 😊

But if you ever want help with your online presence — content, social media, anything — just reply to this email.

Happy to chat when the time is right.

Best,
[YOUR NAME]
```

## QUALIFICATION QUESTIONS

When they reply "yes, interested":

1. "What's your biggest struggle with getting new customers?"
2. "Are you currently doing any marketing? What's working?"
3. "What's your budget range for marketing per month?"
4. "Who handles your social media now?"

## KEY PRINCIPLES

1. **Personalize** — Use their name, business, location
2. **Short emails** — 3-4 sentences max
3. **Ask questions** — Gets them to reply
4. **No hard sell** — Build curiosity first
5. **Follow up 2-3 times** — Then stop

---

## BATCH OUTREACH

Do this in batches:

- **Week 1:** Find 50 businesses, send emails
- **Week 2:** Follow up on 50
- **Week 3:** Find 50 more, send emails
- **Week 4:** Follow up + new batch

Track results in GHL. Measure reply rate.

---

## REPLY RATE BENCHMARKS

- **Cold email avg:** 1-3% reply rate
- **Good outreach:** 5%+ reply rate
- **Great outreach:** 10%+

So for every 100 emails, expect 1-10 replies. That's normal.

The goal: Get them on a call, then sell the service.
